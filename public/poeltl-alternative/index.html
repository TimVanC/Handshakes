The Best Poeltl Alternatives

Updated August 2026 · All free, all daily

Poeltl earned its spot in the morning rotation: eight guesses, one mystery NBA player, and that satisfying cascade of green squares when the division and position lock in. But it's over in a few minutes, and if you got today's guy in three, you want another. These are the games we'd point a Poeltl player to next — starting, in the interest of honesty, with the one we made.

1. Journeyman NBA

NBAMystery playerCareer clues

Poeltl gives you attributes; Journeyman gives you a career. Each day's mystery player is a true journeyman, and his path through the league is revealed one jersey at a time — team colors, number, and the years of each stint, laid out like a spread of trading cards. Name him off two jerseys and you've done something; need all of them and the comment section will know.

Where Poeltl leans current rosters, Journeyman leans the guys who make bar arguments fun: the ones who played for five teams and you can picture in three of the uniforms. There are NFL and MLB dailies too.

2. HoopGrids

NBAGrid

A daily 3×3 basketball grid: each cell is the intersection of two teams (or a team and an award), and you get nine guesses to fill all nine. It tests the opposite muscle from Poeltl — instead of narrowing to one answer, you're producing players from memory who fit the criteria.

3. Crossover Grid

Multi-sportGrid

Same grid idea with a rarity twist — obscure correct answers score better than the obvious superstar, so the game actively rewards deep-roster knowledge. Editions rotate across sports, with the NBA grid a staple.

4. Immaculate Grid (Basketball)

NBAGrid

The Sports Reference original, in its basketball edition. Backed by Basketball Reference's database, its criteria dig deeper than most grids — career stat thresholds, single-season marks, franchise combinations. The most demanding grid on this list, in the best way.

5. Weddle

NFLMystery player

If what you love about Poeltl is the mechanic, Weddle is that mechanic for football: guess the mystery NFL player with feedback on division, position, height, age, and jersey number. A bigger league and deeper depth charts make it a notch harder than its basketball cousin.

6. MLB Pickle

MLBMystery player

The baseball version — nine guesses at a mystery big-leaguer with feedback on team, position, handedness, and birth year. Baseball's massive player pool means the hard days are genuinely hard.

FAQ

What is Poeltl?

A free daily NBA guessing game, now run officially with the National Basketball Players Association and named after center Jakob Poeltl. You get eight guesses at a mystery player; each guess shows whether you matched his team, conference, division, position, height, age, and jersey number.

Is there a Poeltl for other sports?

Yes — Weddle covers the NFL and MLB Pickle covers baseball, both with the same attribute-feedback format. Journeyman runs daily NBA, NFL, and MLB puzzles under one roof if you'd rather keep it in one place.

Which alternative is hardest?

For grids, Immaculate Grid's stat-based criteria are the toughest. For mystery players, Journeyman on a deep-cut day — a career that opens with two obscure one-year stints — will humble anyone.
